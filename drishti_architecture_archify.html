📐 Governing Mathematical Formulation

yolov8n_float16.tflite @ 320x320 (NNAPI Delegate / 4 Threads)

Evaluates 80 COCO classes + 4 bounding box coordinates across 2100 anchor predictions in a single pass.

🛠️ Architectural Role & Protocol Details

Executes single-stage anchor-free convolutional detection on camera frames. Runs in DrishtiService at dynamic intervals (200ms in Navigation, 300ms in Assistant, 600ms in OCR) to conserve battery.

🛡️ Resilience & Fail-Safe Mechanisms

100% offline capacity. Operates independently of cellular connectivity with fallback to 4 CPU threads if NNAPI fails.
